

\section{Conclusion}

We have used simple categorical ideas to construct the eight possibilistic operators introduced by Dubois--Prade in \citeme. We used this to identify some of their properties as arising from abstract nonsense, as well as classified exactly which binary compositions, asymmetric or symmetric, which give closure operators. Finally we introduced infinitely many new closure operators on $P(G)$ by using ideas from category theory, none of which have been studied in the litterature as far as we know. 

The categorical approach open up a new avenue of research, and a new way of understanding existing results. In \citeme, the authors extend the eight possibilistic operators in formal concept analysis to its fuzzy sibling. These results can be obtained in this categorical framework by replacing the boolean category $\2$ by a fuzzy lattice $L$. More generally, by enriching in an epistemic uncertainty framework, as introduced in our previous work \citeme, we can obtain a plethora of extensions to this paper into enriched, uncertain settings. We believe this to be a fruitful direction for future work.

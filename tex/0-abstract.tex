
\begin{abstract}
    \textbf{In this short paper we describe Dubois--Prades eight possibilistic operators in formal concept analysis as arising from Kan extensions and De Morgan duals of the underlying boolean profunctor. This allows us to complete their description of fixedpoints of asymmetric operator compositions, showing in particular that no other compositions give rise to formal concepts outside of the standard Galois connection and the $N\Pi$-pairs.} 
\end{abstract}

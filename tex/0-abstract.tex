
\begin{abstract}
    \textbf{In this short paper we describe Dubois--Prade's eight possibilistic operators in formal concept analysis as arising from Kan extensions and complement duals of the underlying boolean profunctor. This perspective neatly uncovers the underlying reason for the proof that $N\Pi$-pairs form the formal concepts of the complement context. We prove that the Galois connection and the $N\Pi$-pairs are the only symmetric or asymmetric operator compositions that give formal concepts. We also consider categorical tools for constructing new types for concepts not previously studied in the FCA litterature.} 
\end{abstract}

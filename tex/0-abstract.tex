
\begin{abstract}
    \textbf{In this paper we prove that Dubois--Prade's eight possibilistic operators in formal concept analysis arise canonically from Kan extensions and complement duals of the underlying boolean profunctor. This perspective neatly uncovers the underlying reason for the proof that $N\Pi$-pairs form the formal concepts of the complement context. We prove that the FCA closure operator and the $N\Pi$-pairs are the only symmetric or asymmetric operator compositions that give formal concepts. We also use these eight possibilistic operators to construct new closure operators on a formal context, by using standard categorical arguments.} 
\end{abstract}

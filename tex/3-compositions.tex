\section{Compositions}

We have now constructed the eight possibilistic operators in formal concept analysis, as introduced in \citeme, by simple categorical constructions associated to the underlying boolean profunctor $R\: G \nrightarrow G$, and proven that these are interconnected by De Morgan dualities. It remains then, to study the different possible compositions of upper-case and lower-case possibilistic operators -- of particular interest are the asymmetric compositions. 

Given our eight operators, there are $16$ possible compositions that produce an endofunctor on $[G\op, \2]$. As we only consider endofunctors on $[G\op, \2]$ -- the ones on $[M,\2]\op$ are completely analagous -- we will use the combined notation $(-)^\sigma \circ (-)_\rho := (-)^\sigma_\rho$ for simplicity. We then get the following table of compositions:

\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
 & (-)_\Pi & (-)_\Delta & (-)_N & (-)_\nabla \\ \hline
(-)^\Pi
    & (-)^\Pi_\Pi
    & (-)^\Pi_\Delta
    & (-)^\Pi_N
    & (-)^\Pi_\nabla \\[0.3em]

(-)^\Delta
    & (-)^\Delta_\Pi
    & (-)^\Delta_\Delta
    & (-)^\Delta_N
    & (-)^\Delta_\nabla \\[0.3em]

(-)^N
    & (-)^N_\Pi
    & (-)^N_\Delta
    & (-)^N_N
    & (-)^N_\nabla \\[0.3em]

(-)^\nabla
    & (-)^\nabla_\Pi
    & (-)^\nabla_\Delta
    & (-)^\nabla_N
    & (-)^\nabla_\nabla
\end{array}
\]

By our construction of the operators we have two adjunctions associated to the profunctor $R$. We have the FCA closure adjunction,
\begin{center}
\begin{tikzcd}
{[G\op, \2]} \arrow[r, yshift = 2pt, "(-)^\Delta"] & {[M,\2]\op} \arrow[l, yshift = -2pt, "(-)_\Delta"]
\end{tikzcd}
\end{center}
defined by the right Kan extension, and we have the adjunction defined by the left Kan extension,
\begin{center}
\begin{tikzcd}
{[G\op, \2]} \arrow[r, yshift = 2pt, "(-)^\Pi"] & {[M,\2]\op} \arrow[l, yshift = -2pt, "(-)_N"].
\end{tikzcd}
\end{center}
We also have the two adjunctions associated to the complement profunctor $\oR\: G\to M$: 
\begin{center}
\begin{tikzcd}
{[G\op, \2]} \arrow[r, yshift = 2pt, "(-)^\nabla"] & {[M,\2]\op} \arrow[l, yshift = -2pt, "(-)_\nabla"].
\end{tikzcd}
\end{center}
and 
\begin{center}
\begin{tikzcd}
{[G\op, \2]} \arrow[r, yshift = 2pt, "(-)^N"] & {[M,\2]\op} \arrow[l, yshift = -2pt, "(-)_\Pi"].
\end{tikzcd}
\end{center}
